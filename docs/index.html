HID-HOP Web Flasher

Flash the HID-HOP firmware to your Raytac MDBT50Q‑CX‑40 dongle — right from your browser. No toolchain, no compiling.

How it works

1
Enter DFU modeUnplug the dongle. Hold the button and plug it back in while holding. Release after ~1 second. The LED pattern changes to show it's in the bootloader.
2
ConnectClick Connect & Flash and pick the dongle's serial port in the browser prompt (often labelled Open DFU Bootloader or an nRF / SEGGER port).
3
DoneThe firmware streams over, the dongle reboots, and shows up as a USB HID device. That's it.

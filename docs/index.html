HID-HOP Web Flasher

Get the HID-HOP firmware onto your nRF52840 dongle — no toolchain, no compiling. Pick your dongle below.

Which dongle do you have?

Two kinds of nRF52840 dongles, two ways to flash. Not sure? If plugging it in while double-tapping a reset shows up as a USB drive, it's drag-and-drop (Option 1).

Option 1 · Drag-and-drop a .uf2

For Seeed XIAO nRF52840, Makerdiary MDK, and other Adafruit-bootloader dongles. Can't really go wrong — it's just copying a file.

1
Download the firmware⬇ hid-hop-xiao.uf2
2
Enter bootloaderPlug the dongle in and double-tap the RESET button. A USB drive appears (e.g. XIAO-SENSE / NRF52BOOT).
3
Drag it onCopy hid-hop-xiao.uf2 onto that drive. The dongle flashes, reboots automatically, and comes up as a USB HID device. Done.

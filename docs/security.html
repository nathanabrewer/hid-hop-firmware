Why a key beats a password

A tiny dongle that won't be fooled.

HID-HOP signs you in with FIDO2, the same standard behind hardware security keys and passkeys. Your login is cryptographically tied to the real website, so a look-alike page gets nothing it can use.

Phishing bounces off

Fake sign-in pages can't reuse what the key signs. No codes to type, nothing to trick out of you.

Lost it? Wipe it.

Erase the keys from your phone when the dongle is in range. Most security keys can't do that.

One press to approve

Every sign-in needs a physical press, so malware can't log in as you silently.

No hype

What Lite is, and what it isn't.

We'd rather you buy the right key than the wrong one.

  • Real protection against phishing and password leaks, the attacks that hit most people
  • Keys generated on the dongle and never sent to your phone
  • Remote wipe, open firmware and 8 separate identities
  • Not a secure element: someone who gets the dongle in their hands with lab tools could extract keys
  • Not yet certified or independently audited
  • Always register a second key as a backup
